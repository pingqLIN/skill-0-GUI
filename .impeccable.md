## Design Context

### Users
- Primary users are reviewers and operators inspecting Skill-0 parser output before approval, export, or external sharing.
- They work in a focused desktop context with dense information: intake evidence, parser status, validation, consistency, review notes, and sign-off all need to stay visible without constant context switching.
- The main job is to move from intake to trustworthy review decisions with traceability, not to admire visualizations or browse a generic analytics dashboard.

### Brand Personality
- Reviewer-first
- Honest
- Operational

The interface should feel rigorous, controlled, and credible. It should communicate that parser mode, evidence quality, and review status matter. It should not feel playful, vague, or overly consumer-polished.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dense editorial workbench rather than a card-heavy dashboard
- Light mode first, with warm neutral surfaces and dark slate accents
- High information bandwidth, restrained motion, strong typography, and explicit grouping

References inferred from the product:
- review console
- evidence desk
- operator cockpit

Anti-references:
- generic KPI dashboard
- marketing landing page
- decorative glassmorphism everywhere
- novelty 3D-first storytelling

### Design Principles
1. Preserve the product sequence: intake -> analysis -> review -> export.
2. Keep high-value truth visible: parser mode, evidence status, validation state, and review decision should remain in view.
3. Increase density through structure, not clutter: use docked panels, tabs, and scannable metrics instead of nested cards.
4. Let typography do the hierarchy heavy lifting before color or decoration.
5. Preserve all major existing functions, but reorganize them into a clearer reviewer-first shell.
